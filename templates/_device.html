Why net and gross are both shown. Netting every client together hides offsetting errors: in July 2026 the month totals differed by only −0.4% while individual advertisers were out by +33%, because inflation on some clients cancelled a 304k-impression shortfall on others. This report compares every client separately and reports the gross error, which is the number that matters. A client whose impressions and clicks are both up by the same factor is flagged as the duplicated-rows fingerprint — the signature of a rolling export pooled without de-duplication.
