Blank creative names (delivery the export can't attribute to a creative — sorted by impressions at risk) CSV

Known cause (checked against AdLib's own S3 files, Aug 2026). AdLib's Campaign Creative Report carries a creative name on 100% of rows for the same window — the blank is introduced in the TapClicks ingestion, which collapses a campaign's creatives into one nameless row. The impressions, clicks and spend on that row equal the campaign's true total, so delivery figures are correct; only the creative attribution is lost. Treat rows here as "spend we can't tie to a creative", not as inventory that failed to run.

UTM tagging (counted by distinct {{ us.id_basis }} — {{ '{:,.0f}'.format(us.creatives) }} creatives across {{ '{:,.0f}'.format(us.placements) }} creative × campaign placements) CSV

"Tagged" means the clickthrough URL carries at least one utm_* parameter. Fully tagged means it carries all three of utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign — anything less and Google Analytics can't attribute the session to the campaign. Where a creative runs several URLs, the verdict comes from the URL behind the most delivery.
